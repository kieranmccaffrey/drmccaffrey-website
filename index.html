About

There for you at every stage

Dr McCaffrey manages both normal and high‑risk pregnancies, alongside a strong interest in advanced minimally invasive gynaecological surgery. He has been appointed a Consultant VMO at Royal North Shore Hospital (Public), North Shore Private, The Mater Hospital and St Vincent's Hospital.

Outside the clinic, he volunteers with Street Side Medics, providing healthcare to people experiencing homelessness, Surf Life Saving Clubs and serves as a Medical Officer with the Army Reserve.

Meet Dr McCaffrey

A short introduction, in his own words.

Dr McCaffrey trained through Royal North Shore Hospital and completed a Master of Surgery at the University of Sydney, with fellowship training in minimally invasive gynaecological surgery at St Vincent's Hospital, Darlinghurst.

Manages both normal and high-risk pregnancies:

Dr McCaffrey is able to deliver your baby at either the most prestigious private hospitals or a major tertiary public hospital capable of managing pre‑term birth and complex pregnancies.

Experienced in advanced surgical procedures:

Including being extensively trained using the Da Vinci robotic surgical system to manage advanced cases of endometriosis, heavy menstrual bleeding and complex hysterectomy. Dr McCaffrey has been designated a specialist proctor and is flown interstate to train other surgeons.

Provides holistic women's health care:

Dr McCaffrey integrates midwives, lactation consultants, women's pelvic floor physiotherapists, naturopaths and allied health into his practice.

Areas of care: Obstetrics & Gynaecology

Two disciplines, one continuity of care.

Whether you're navigating a pregnancy or a gynaecological condition, care is delivered personally — the same doctor, from first consult through to delivery or recovery.

Obstetrics

Pregnancy & birth

Pregnancy is a unique and special time. Dr McCaffrey advocates for normal birth, and holds the training and expertise to manage pregnancies complicated by high‑risk obstetric and medical conditions.

Birth is a personal experience — informed choice, and a safe, happy outcome for mother and baby, guide every decision.

Gynaecology

Minimally invasive surgery

Extensively trained on the Da Vinci robotic‑assisted surgical system, Dr McCaffrey treats complex pelvic conditions — including endometriosis and heavy menstrual bleeding — and serves as a surgical proctor for other specialists.

Women's Health, holistically

Great outcomes rely on more than the delivery room — a trusted circle of allied health professionals who work alongside Dr McCaffrey throughout pregnancy and beyond. Our midwife and women's health physiotherapist both consult out of Dr McCaffrey's rooms, working alongside him to support you.

Tahlia : Wolfe & Cub

Midwifery support before & after birth

Ongoing midwifery input before and after delivery makes a real difference to how supported you feel through pregnancy and the fourth trimester. Tahlia runs boutique, personalised antenatal classes and breastfeeding / lactation support, alongside daily bulk billed — free — midwifery telehealth support.

Kate Leslie

Women's Health Physiotherapy

Good management of the pelvic floor matters both during pregnancy and in the treatment of pelvic pain conditions. Kate provides specialised women's health physiotherapy to support patients through these stages with expert, individualised care.
